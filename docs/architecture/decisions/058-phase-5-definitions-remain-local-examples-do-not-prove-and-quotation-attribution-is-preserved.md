# ADR 058: Phase 5 definitions remain local, examples do not prove, and quotation attribution is preserved

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-07-28  
Phase: 5

## Decision

Canonical definition acts produce local definition records containing the
defined expression, exact defining text, definition type, declared or source
utterance scope, applicable context, explicit exclusions, competing
definitions, definition-challenge links, confidence, and review state.
Definitions cannot become globally reusable without later evidence.

Canonical example acts produce records with exact example spans, typed example
form, real/hypothetical/quoted/uncertain status, bounded candidate
generalizations, temporal and participant references where structural,
confidence, and review. A nearest prior generalization is only probable;
several remain alternatives; none remains unresolved. Example records
literally prohibit representativeness and proof findings.

Canonical quotation acts produce quotation-use records. When matching Phase 4
evidence exists, the record copies the quoted span, acoustic attribution,
acoustic speaker, quoting speaker, attributed speaker, original-source
reference, attribution text, and embedded-source references. Phase 5 cannot
mutate acoustic attribution. Unmatched quotation acts remain review-required.

## Consequences

- Definition meaning and reuse remain bounded by declared discourse scope.
- Competing definitions remain peers rather than being silently resolved.
- Example-to-generalization links do not establish proof or
  representativeness.
- Quotation use remains distinct from who is acoustically speaking.
- Phase 4 quotation evidence remains authoritative for quotation structure and
  acoustic attribution.
