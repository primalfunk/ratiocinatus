# Phase 5 objection, rebuttal, concession, and qualification construction

Status: **PASSED**

Canonical objection and rebuttal acts now produce bounded relations with typed
challenge dimensions or rebuttal methods, exact supporting and challenged
spans, target acts and utterances, qualifications, alternatives, context,
temporal distance, confidence, review, and unresolved issues.

Concessions preserve exact conceded material and retained restrictions.
Qualifications preserve dimension, scope, condition, exception, and ambiguous
same-utterance targets. Temporal proximity produces only probable reviewable
targets. Rebuttal classification never establishes success, and no factual
adjudication or intent inference is performed.
