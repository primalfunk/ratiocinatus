## Boundary

An utterance is a derived analytical grouping, not necessarily a sentence,
speaker turn, transcript segment, complete thought, or proposition. A quoted
speaker is not necessarily the acoustic speaker. Interruption structure does
not establish intent or blame. Unknown attribution remains acceptable.

This slice does not claim that utterance segmentation has been implemented or
evaluated. It introduces no claim extraction, argument analysis, factual
adjudication, credibility scoring, intent inference, or participant judgment.
