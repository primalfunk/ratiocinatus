# Phase 5 question-answer construction

Status: **PASSED**

Canonical question acts now produce source-grounded question artifacts with
exact spans, structural form, alternatives, surface presupposition markers,
scope, confidence, and conservative addressee state. Canonical answer acts
produce bounded relations that preserve answer form, explicitness, polarity,
qualification, premise rejection, deferral, refusal, inability, ambiguity,
co-answer structure, confidence, and review.

A single preceding question in the exact bounded-temporal context is only a
probable reviewable target. Multiple candidates remain alternatives, and an
unsupported target remains unresolved. No responsiveness, adequacy,
completeness, evasion, fairness, loadedness, or answerability judgment is
introduced.
